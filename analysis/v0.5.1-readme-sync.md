## Selected Q&A

### Q1

Why keep Exp5 as a separate README section instead of merging it into Exp4?

### A1

Because Exp5 is not just a parameter tweak. It changes the acquisition story from a single-path free-`XYZ` BA to a UAV-style multi-strip oblique block, and that deserves its own experimental interpretation.

### Q2

Why mention the `z-down` convention in the README?

### A2

Because without that note, the scene figure and the pose parameterization would look inconsistent. The convention is part of why Exp5 fairly tests the staged story instead of accidentally breaking it with artificial near-`180 deg` ground-truth tilt.
